1Fridge Manager — five screens from Spec v3
Assumptions & reasoning
What I took from the spec

Desktop-only static screens for §8.1–§8.5. Every field, filter, badge and action on these screens is named in the spec; nothing was invented. Today is treated as 3 September 2026, so the seeded dates produce two Expired and four Expiring-soon items (§6.4). Capacity figures are internally consistent: 47 of 65 units used, 23 active items, and Jonas Weber sits exactly at his quota of 3 — the two conditions §9 asks for so the guards can be demonstrated.

Where I had to decide

Classical is a mono palette with no danger role, so Expired is drawn as a deep oxblood stroke — the only colour added, used as an outline and never as a fill. Bootstrap card/badge conventions are replaced by the system's bordered .card and tinted .tag. Capacity is drawn as a schematic elevation on the dashboard — a reading aid, not a drag target.
